== TITLE
Memorize: Lines, Speech, Poems
== SHORT
Learn lines, poems and speeches hands-free, with a prompter when you get stuck.
== NOTES
Now in 14 languages: Hindi, Chinese, Japanese and Korean added.
== FULL
Play lines, a poem for school, a speech or a presentation… Memorize Assistant reads your text to you, listens when it's your turn and prompts you like a stage prompter when you get stuck.

🎭 PLAYS AND LINES
• Paste your script or import it from TXT, PDF, Word or a photo; characters and lines are split automatically
• Choose your character; the others are read in different voices
• Set the tone of a line (angry, sad, whisper…) or record the other parts in your own voice

🎙️ HANDS-FREE REHEARSAL
• When it's your turn the app listens; say your line and it carries on when you stop
• Stuck? The prompter whispers the first words, then the whole line if needed
• Practise without touching your phone – while walking or commuting

📜 POEMS AND TEXTS
• Build up: line 1, then 1–2, then 1–3…
• Progressive hiding: more words disappear after every perfect round
• Recite from memory: it stops where you skip or say something wrong and gives you a report
• Remembers the lines you struggle with and lets you practise just those

🎤 PRESENTATIONS
• Page by page: a time for every slide, with a voice cue when time is up
• Timer, target time and speaking pace
• Key-word hints to learn the flow instead of reading word by word

🔒 PRIVACY
• No account, no ads
• Everything runs on your phone: your texts and recordings never leave it
• 14 languages
